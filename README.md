## What Is This?

A conversational AI agent that:
1. **Interviews you** about what you want to learn
2. **Researches the subject** to understand scope and complexity
3. **Designs custom curriculum** based on your goals and schedule
4. **Generates daily learning materials** (podcasts, exercises, resources)
5. **Guides you through** with daily support and accountability
6. **Adapts in real-time** based on your progress

The result: A structured path from where you are to where you want to be - for literally any subject.

## How It Works: The NotebookLM Engine

**NotebookLM is the secret weapon** that makes this system powerful. Here's the daily workflow:

### Step 1: Research Prompt Generation
Claude generates a custom NotebookLM research prompt based on:
- Your specific goal and context
- Your current knowledge level
- The day's topic
- Your application (work, project, etc.)

### Step 2: Source Upload
You upload relevant sources to NotebookLM:
- Framework documents
- Academic papers
- Industry reports
- Technical documentation

### Step 3: Podcast Generation
NotebookLM's Audio Overview feature creates a 20-30 minute **conversational podcast** between two AI hosts discussing the topic - customized with YOUR context and goals baked in.

### Step 4: Listen & Learn
Download the podcast and listen during:
- Commutes
- Workouts
- Household tasks
- Anywhere you can't read

### Step 5: Apply & Reflect
Complete daily exercises and capture learnings in your Notion workspace.

**Why this works:** NotebookLM turns dense research documents into engaging conversations that speak directly to YOUR situation. It's like having two expert tutors discussing exactly what you need to learn.

## The Daily Workflow

Each day follows this proven 8-step process:

**Step 1: NotebookLM Research Prompt**
- Claude generates a custom prompt with your context
- Lists specific sources to upload (frameworks, papers, docs)

**Step 2: NotebookLM Podcast Synthesis**
- Upload sources to NotebookLM
- Paste the synthesis prompt
- Generate 20-30 minute Audio Overview

**Step 3: Download & Transcribe**
- Download the .m4a podcast file
- (Optional) Transcribe with Whisper for searchable text

**Step 4: Listen**
- Audio learning during commutes, workouts, etc.
- Conversational format makes complex topics engaging

**Step 5: Study Sheet**
- Extract 3 key concepts
- Note 3 resources to go deeper
- Identify 2 common misconceptions

**Step 6: Daily Exercise**
- Hands-on practice using your real context
- Build something, solve a problem, apply concepts

**Step 7: End of Day Debrief**
- Capture 3 things you learned
- Note what's still unclear
- Connect to your goal

**Step 8: Track Progress**
- Mark day complete in Notion
- See your momentum build

**Total time:** 30-90 minutes/day depending on your commitment

## What is NotebookLM?

[NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com) is Google's AI research assistant that powers the learning content generation.

**Why NotebookLM is critical:**

🎙️ **Audio Overview Feature** - Generates podcast-style conversations between two AI hosts
📚 **Source Grounding** - Uses your uploaded documents as the knowledge base
🎯 **Context Awareness** - When you provide custom prompts, it tailors the conversation
🔬 **Research Quality** - Pulls from authoritative sources you provide, not generic internet content

**The magic:** Instead of reading dry documentation, you get two engaging hosts discussing the topic in conversation - with YOUR context, YOUR goals, and YOUR application built into every discussion.

**Free to use:** NotebookLM is currently free. You just need a Google account.
